## Detailed description

*Discovery → App Directory → Detailed description* (Markdown).

```markdown
## 🍅 Focus together, right in your voice call

**Pomopensource** is a simple pomodoro timer for Discord. Launch it in a voice call and everyone shares **one timer**: focus together, take breaks together, and get back to work as a group.

### ✨ What you get

**One timer for the whole call**
- Anyone in the call can start, pause or switch between focus and breaks
- The call sets the rhythm for everyone: 25, 50, 90 minutes… with a long break every 4 pomodoros
- See who's focusing with you
- Your Discord status shows you're focusing, with the time left
- Stays visible in picture-in-picture and grid view while you chat

**Your focus time, saved**
- Signed in with your Discord account, no extra signup
- Every pomodoro is saved to **your** account, even in a group
- Hours focused, days active and your current streak
- A calendar of your week, month and year
- Note what you're working on, or track time per project

**Just for you**
- 12 backgrounds to pick from
- 10 alert sounds, with volume, or none at all

### How to start
1. Join a voice channel
2. Click the **Activities** button (the rocket) 🚀
3. Pick **Pomopensource** and press start

### Free and open source
No ads, nothing to pay. The app only gets your basic Discord profile, never your messages or servers.
- Website, same account: https://pomopensource.corentindupaigne.com
- Source code (MIT): https://github.com/corentin-dupaigne/pomopensource
- Bug or idea? Open an issue on GitHub.
```
